Hauz Khas Village

4 of your saves are here · 2 not tried
The take

A crumbling medieval complex with a lake at the bottom and a lane of cafes on top. Come for the ruins at golden hour, stay because the coffee is genuinely good.

Known for
the ruins rooftop cafes the lake design shops
Things to do
Walk the deer park before the cafes open — it is empty until about 10.
Blue Tokai for the pour-over, upstairs, plugs at every table.
Kunzum if you want a bookshop with the coffee.
Good to know — the main lane floods in monsoon and parking is hopeless after 7pm. Auto to the gate.
